Connect to your TV

On Nuvix, open Settings → Manage on phone or laptop and start a browser session.

Add a provider

Add an Xtream, M3U or Stalker portal from here — a real keyboard beats the TV remote. Details pass through Cloudflare over HTTPS and are saved on the TV.

Point recordings at a network drive. Saved straight to your TV — the password stays on the TV.
