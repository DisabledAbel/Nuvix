Nuvix — manage your TV

Changes apply to your TV the moment you make them.

Connect to your TV

On Nuvix, open Settings → Manage on phone or laptop and start a browser session.

No QR code or account needed. Codes expire in 5 minutes.

Add a provider

Add an Xtream, M3U or Stalker portal from here — a real keyboard beats the TV remote. Details pass through Cloudflare over HTTPS and are saved on the TV.

Recording to NAS (SMB)

Point recordings at a network drive. Saved straight to your TV — the password stays on the TV.

Tap a name to rename. Star to favourite, eye to hide, arrows to reorder.

Cloudflare relays channel details and changes to your TV over HTTPS. Provider and NAS details you submit pass through the service; they are saved on the TV and are not stored by this manager. This is not end-to-end encryption. Only connect to a manager service you trust. Refresh or close this tab to forget the browser token; start a new session on the TV to reconnect.
